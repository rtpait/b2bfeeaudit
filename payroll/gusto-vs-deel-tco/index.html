Frequently Asked Questions (2026 Verified Data)

Is Gusto or Deel cheaper for a US startup with international contractors?

Many CFOs run a hybrid stack: Gusto Simple ($49 base + $6/seat) for US W-2 employees and domestic US 1099s, paired with Deel ($49/mo per international contractor or $599/mo per EOR employee) strictly for cross-border hires.

What is the hidden FX spread on Deel and Gusto Global EOR invoices?

When funding foreign EOR payroll in USD while employees are paid in EUR, GBP, CAD, or INR, EOR providers apply a 1.25% to 2.5% FX conversion spread over the mid-market interbank rate. On $100,000 of international payroll, a 1.5% FX spread adds $1,500/yr.

Does Deel handle multi-state US payroll tax registrations?

Yes. Deel US Payroll ($19 PEPM) supports 50-state payroll tax filing and embedded state unemployment insurance (SUI) registration.

What are the mandatory 2026 federal and Texas payroll taxes for 15 W-2 employees?

Per IRS Publication 15 and the Texas Workforce Commission (TWC), employers pay 7.65% FICA, $42/employee FUTA (0.6% on $7,000), and $243/employee Texas SUI (2.7% on $9,000), totaling $4,275/yr in statutory unemployment taxes before FICA.
