Frequently Asked Questions (2026 Verified Data)

How much do Deel and Remote actually cost per EOR employee in 2026?

Both Deel and Remote charge $599/month per EOR employee on annual plans ($699/month month-to-month), plus statutory foreign employer social contributions (which range from 12% in Canada to 45% in France) and a 1.25%–2.0% FX currency conversion spread.

Why is Remote $20/month cheaper per international contractor than Deel?

Remote prices international contractor management at $29/contractor/month (active invoicing), whereas Deel's standard contractor tier is $49/contractor/month (offering crypto/Wise instant withdrawal options and advance pay).

At how many employees in one country is a local subsidiary cheaper than an EOR?

At $599/mo ($7,188/yr) per seat, 5 EOR employees in one country cost $35,940/yr in EOR fees. Setting up and maintaining a local foreign subsidiary typically costs $18,000–$30,000/yr, making the EOR-to-entity break-even threshold 4 to 6 employees in a single country.

What IRS form is required when paying non-US international contractors?

Per IRS instructions (.gov), US businesses do not issue Form 1099-NEC to non-US citizens performing work outside the United States; instead, you must collect and retain IRS Form W-8BEN (for individuals) or W-8BEN-E (for foreign entities).
