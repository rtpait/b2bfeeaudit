Frequently Asked Questions (2026 Verified Data)

Does switching from Shopify Payments to an external Interchange-Plus gateway save money?

Inside the native Shopify web cart on non-Plus plans, Shopify's 0.6%–2.0% third-party transaction fee usually offsets IC+ savings unless your plan is Shopify Advanced (0.6% fee) with high debit/high-ticket volume. However, moving B2B wholesale, phone, and invoiced orders off-cart to Stax or Helcim saves 1.0%–1.5% immediately with zero Shopify penalty.

When does upgrading from Shopify Basic ($39/mo, 2.9%) to Shopify Advanced ($399/mo, 2.5%) break even?

The $360/month subscription increase between Basic ($39/mo) and Advanced ($399/mo) divided by the 0.40% card rate reduction (2.9% vs. 2.5%) breaks even at exactly $90,000/month in domestic online card sales.

What if Shopify Payments freezes my payouts or bans my product category?

Because Shopify Payments runs on Stripe's prohibited-business list, high-ticket B2B equipment, supplements, vape/CBD, and credit-repair merchants require a dedicated underwritten MID via PaymentCloud (connected to Shopify via Authorize.Net or NMI).

How do regulated debit cards impact e-commerce processing margins?

Under Federal Reserve Reg II (.gov), regulated debit transactions cost 0.05% + $0.22 wholesale regardless of order size. On a $140 order, wholesale debit interchange is $0.29 vs. $4.36 on Shopify Basic (2.9% + $0.30).
