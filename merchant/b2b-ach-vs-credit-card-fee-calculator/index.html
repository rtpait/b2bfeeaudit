Key 2026 Hidden-Fee & Statutory Audit Findings

  • What Is Level 2 and Level 3 Credit Card Processing? Visa and Mastercard charge ~2.95% (Level 1) on corporate, purchasing, and government cards when only the card number and ZIP are sent. When Stax or Helcim automatically injects Level 2/3 fields (sales tax amount, customer code, commodity code, invoice number), Visa/MC drop the wholesale rate to ~1.90%–2.10%.
  • Why High-Ticket B2B Invoices Bleed on Uncapped Percentage Rails: Paying 3.70% on a $2,500 B2B invoice costs $92.80. Paying via Helcim ACH (0.50% capped at $6.00) costs $6.00—an $86.80 savings on a single invoice.
  • Automated Credit Card Surcharging / Convenience Fee Option: Both Stax and Helcim offer compliant B2B invoice portals that let customers choose between Free ACH or a 3.0% credit card surcharge.

Frequently Asked Questions (2026 Verified Data)

How much does Level 2 and Level 3 processing save on corporate credit cards?

Automated Level 2 and Level 3 data enrichment reduces Visa and Mastercard commercial/corporate interchange rates by 0.50% to 0.90% on every B2B card transaction ($4,500 to $8,100/year saved on $75,000/month in corporate card volume).

Does Stripe automatically pass Level 2/3 savings back to merchants on the 2.9% plan?

No. On Stripe's standard 2.9% + $0.30 blended plan, you pay 2.9% + $0.30 (plus +0.8% on Stripe Invoicing) regardless of whether the transaction qualifies for lower Level 2/3 wholesale interchange.

How do Helcim and Stripe compare on B2B ACH bank transfer fees?

Helcim charges 0.50% capped at $6.00 per ACH transaction with $0 invoicing surcharge. Stripe charges 0.80% capped at $5.00, but if sent via Stripe Invoicing Plus, adds its +0.80% invoicing software fee unless configured via standalone Checkout.

Are B2B ACH transfers regulated by the Federal Reserve?

Yes. Commercial ACH clearing operates over the Federal Reserve FedACH network (.gov) and Nacha operating rules, providing next-day settlement at a fraction of card network interchange.
