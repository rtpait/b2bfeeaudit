Frequently Asked Questions (2026 Verified Data)

At what monthly volume should a business switch from Stripe to Helcim?

Because Helcim has a $0 monthly account fee and no early termination fee, businesses begin saving money immediately at $10,000–$15,000/month in card volume, with savings reaching $5,000–$12,000+/yr between $40,000 and $100,000/month.

Why does Stripe cost 3.6%–4.1% effective when its advertised rate is 2.9% + $0.30?

Stripe's 2.9% + $0.30 base applies only to domestic US self-serve checkout. Adding Stripe Invoicing (+0.80%), Stripe Billing (+0.50%–0.70%), international cards (+1.50%), currency conversion (+1.00%), and non-refundable fee policies on refunds pushes real effective rates to 3.6%–4.1%.

How does Federal Reserve Regulation II (Durbin Amendment) lower Helcim fees?

Per the Federal Reserve Board (.gov), debit cards issued by banks with >$10B in assets are capped at 0.05% + $0.21 (+$0.01 fraud adjustment). Interchange-Plus processors like Helcim pass that 0.05% + $0.22 rate directly to the merchant, whereas flat-rate processors pocket the spread.

Does Helcim offer an API and QuickBooks Online sync to replace Stripe?

Yes. Helcim provides HelcimPay.js, REST APIs, hosted B2B invoice portals, automated Level 2/Level 3 commercial card data enrichment, and 2-way QuickBooks Online reconciliation.
