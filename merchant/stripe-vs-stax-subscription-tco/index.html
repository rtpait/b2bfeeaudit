Frequently Asked Questions (2026 Verified Data)

What is the exact monthly volume where Stax beats Stripe and Helcim?

Stax ($99/mo + 0% markup) becomes cheaper than Stripe (2.9% + $0.30) at approximately $18,500/month in card volume, and becomes cheaper than Helcim ($0/mo + 0.35%–0.40% markup) at approximately $34,000/month.

Does Stax really charge 0% markup over Visa and Mastercard interchange?

Yes. Stax passes through 100% of wholesale Visa, Mastercard, Discover, and Amex interchange and card-brand assessment fees at cost, charging a flat monthly subscription ($99–$199/mo) plus $0.08 (card-present) or $0.15 (online/keyed) per transaction.

How do Level 2 and Level 3 interchange rates save B2B merchants money on Stax?

When another business pays you with a corporate purchasing card, standard Level 1 interchange is 2.70%–2.95%. Passing Level 2/3 line-item data via Stax or Helcim qualifies the transaction for B2B interchange rates that are 0.50% to 0.90% lower.

How are debit card fees regulated under Federal Reserve Reg II?

Under Federal Reserve Regulation II (.gov), covered issuers may charge no more than 0.05% of the transaction value plus $0.21 (and $0.01 fraud-prevention adjustment). On Stax, you pay that exact wholesale debit cap plus $0.08–$0.15.
