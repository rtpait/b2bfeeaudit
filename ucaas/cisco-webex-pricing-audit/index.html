⏱️ 30-Second CFO Audit Verdict: Cisco Webex 2026 Pricing & TCO Verified Oct 2026 Rate Card
  • 🏆 BEST VALUE TIER: WEBEX SUITE ($22.50/MO)
    At $22.50/user/mo annual ($25.00/mo monthly), Webex Suite saves $7.50/seat/mo (-25%) compared to buying Webex Meet ($12) + Webex Calling ($17) separately, and beats unbundled Microsoft Teams + Teams Phone + AI Recap ($29–$49/seat/mo) and Google Workspace Standard + Voice ($34/seat/mo).

  • ⚡ BUNDLED AI ADVANTAGE ($0 EXTRA)
    Webex includes Webex AI Assistant (meeting recaps, action items, call summaries, and real-time translation) in paid plans at $0 extra—saving $3,000 to $9,000/year for a 25-seat team vs. Microsoft Teams Premium/Copilot.
